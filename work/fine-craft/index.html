Atelier Nine

A jewellery storefront where craft has to be visible before price is discussed.

◆Concept study — a self-initiated exploration, not client work

01 The brief we set

A jewellery storefront where craft has to be visible before price is discussed.

Concept studies are how we work out a category before a client asks us to. We take a sector, set ourselves the constraints a real brief would have, and design against them.

02 What we worked out

Craft at full resolution

Finishing and material are the product. Imagery served large enough to reward inspection is the whole argument.

Provenance in the decision

Metal, stone and origin are criteria, not fine print. They sit beside the price.

Reassurance at payment

High-value baskets need guarantees and returns policy exactly where the hesitation happens.
