Aurelo

◆Concept study — a self-initiated exploration, not client work

01 The brief we set

A jewellery storefront where the product has to carry weight before the price does.

Concept studies are how we work out a category before a client asks us to. We take a sector, set ourselves the constraints a real brief would have, and design against them. No client, no deadline, and nothing to lose by trying the harder answer first.

02 What we worked out

Photography leads, chrome follows

At this price point the customer decides on the imagery. Every interface element earns its place or gets out of the way of the product shot.

Certification made visible

Provenance, carat and metal are decision criteria, not fine print. They sit in the buying decision, not in a tab three clicks down.

A considered checkout

High-value baskets need reassurance at the moment of payment, not friction removal. Guarantees and returns policy appear where the doubt appears.
