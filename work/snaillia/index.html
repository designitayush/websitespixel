01 The situation

Single-product skincare brands live or die on one page. There is no catalogue to browse and no collection to get lost in — the product page is the shop.

So the build put everything a buyer needs in one scroll: what it is, who it is for, how it is made, and what it arrives in.

02 What we focused on

The product page is the store

Size selection and add-to-cart sit immediately under the proposition. No hunting, no second page, no reason to leave and think about it.

Manufacturing and packaging as sales pages

Natural skincare buyers care how a product is made. Giving Manufacturing and Packaging their own places in the navigation turns due diligence into an argument for buying.

Restraint as a signal

A calm green field, one product, generous space. In this category a busy page reads as a cheap product.
