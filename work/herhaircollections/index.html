“From scratch to now I’m making $10K a month revenue.”

HerHaircollectionsVerified review · March 2025
01 The situation

The brand came to us with products, an audience and no way to sell online. Not a redesign, not a migration — nothing existed yet. That is the hardest brief to get right, because every decision is the first one and there is no data to argue with.

The work was a full Shopify store build: the storefront, the product experience, and the path from landing to checkout.

02 What we focused on

Built to sell on the first visit

Hair extensions are a considered purchase with a lot of variables — length, texture, origin. The product experience had to answer those questions before the customer had to ask them.

A storefront the founder can run alone

A new brand cannot wait on a developer to change a banner. The store was built so the day-to-day belongs to the founder, not to us.

Nothing decorative in the way of the sale

Every element on the path to checkout had to earn its place. What does not help the customer buy was left out.

03 The outcome

The founder reports the store is now doing $10,000 a month in revenue. That figure is theirs, given in a verified review on our Shopify Partner profile — not a number we calculated for ourselves.
