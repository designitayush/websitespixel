All prices in USD. Fixed price agreed in writing before any work starts — the number you approve is the number you pay.

What moves the price up.

Being honest about this builds more trust than a low headline number does. If your project has these, expect to be above the from-price.

  • More than 500 products
  • Custom app or ERP integrations
  • B2B or wholesale pricing rules
  • Subscriptions and recurring orders
  • Multi-currency or multi-region storefronts
  • More than five genuinely unique page templates

What is not included.

Preventing a surprise later is worth more to us than winning a project we would lose your goodwill on.

  • Copywriting and content production
  • Product photography and video
  • Paid advertising management and ad spend
  • Your Shopify subscription
  • Third-party app subscriptions

Payment terms. 50% to start, 50% at launch. Larger builds can be split across three milestones instead — just ask on the call.

Questions about money.

Why “from” and not a fixed number?

Because a store with 40 products and one template is genuinely less work than one with 4,000 and a wholesale portal. What is fixed is that you get one number, in writing, before anything starts — and it does not move unless you ask for something new.

What if my project does not fit a package?

Most do not exactly. The packages exist so you can place yourself in the right range. On the call we scope your actual project and quote that.

Do you take payment plans?

50/50 is standard. Three milestones on larger builds. We will not start work before the first payment, and you will not be asked for the last one before you have seen it live.

What happens if I want changes after launch?

Launch builds include a support window — two weeks on Launch, thirty days on Rebuild. After that, either pay for the work or move onto Grow, which covers it monthly.

Do I own everything?

Yes. The code, the design files and every account are yours, in your Shopify admin. No lock-in, no licence, nothing you have to keep paying us to keep using.

What if we are not a good fit?

We will say so on the call and point you somewhere better. That happens a few times a year and it has never once been the wrong decision.

Get a real number for your store.

Thirty minutes. We look at what you have, tell you which range you are in and why, and you decide from there. No pitch deck.
