  • Quiet month? Pause the plan. You keep everything already shipped and pick it up when you need it.

Not included in the $699

  • Advertising spend
  • Third-party app and software fees
  • Premium themes and external subscriptions
  • Major migrations and large standalone projects — quoted separately
  • Unlimited work — the plan is capped at 15 hours a month
  • Guaranteed rankings, traffic, sales, or revenue — nobody can honestly promise those

Growth is a working partnership, not unlimited work. We would rather tell you plainly what fifteen hours buys than promise everything and quietly deliver less.

What’s quoted separately.

Launch and Rebuild cover a defined scope. If your project has any of these, we quote the extra work in writing before anything starts.

  • More than 500 products
  • Custom app or ERP integrations
  • B2B or wholesale pricing rules
  • Subscriptions and recurring orders
  • Multi-currency or multi-region storefronts
  • More than five genuinely unique page templates

What is not included.

Preventing a surprise later is worth more to us than winning a project we would lose your goodwill on.

  • Copywriting and content production
  • Product photography and video
  • Paid advertising management and ad spend
  • Your Shopify subscription
  • Third-party app subscriptions and premium themes

Payment terms. Launch and Rebuild: 50% to start, 50% at launch. Larger, separately quoted builds can be split across three milestones — just ask on the call. Growth is billed monthly.

Questions about money.

Is the Shopify Launch price a one-time payment?

Yes — $1,499, one-time, to design, build, and launch your store. It’s not a subscription, and it includes 30 days of post-launch technical support. Your Shopify plan and any paid apps or premium themes are billed separately by those providers.

What’s included in the Shopify Rebuild package?

A full redesign of your existing store ($1,999, one-time): audit, complete storefront redesign, custom theme work, redesigned homepage, improved product and collection pages, navigation and UX improvements, mobile optimization, performance work, on-page SEO improvements, and conversion-focused refinements — plus launch support and 30 days of post-launch technical support. Scope is agreed in writing before we start.

What’s included in the $699/month Growth plan?

A monthly partnership giving you access to our full range of Shopify services — development, store management, SEO, ecommerce marketing support, CRO, and analytics — delivered through up to 15 hours of agreed work each month, prioritized together. It’s built so you can draw on multiple skill sets without hiring separate specialists.

Can I request development, SEO, and marketing in the same month?

Yes. All service categories are available within your monthly allowance. We plan the month together and focus on one primary workstream at a time so each piece is done properly.

Is there a limit to monthly development work?

Yes, and we’re upfront about it: up to 15 hours of agreed work per billing month. It’s not unlimited. If a month needs more, we scope and quote the extra work for your approval first — no surprise charges.

Are advertising budgets included?

No. Ad spend (Meta, Google, etc.) is paid directly by you to the platform and isn’t part of the $699 fee. The plan can include campaign support and tracking; the media budget itself is separate.

Are Shopify and third-party app subscriptions included?

No. Your Shopify subscription, paid apps, premium themes, and external software are billed by those providers. We’ll recommend cost-effective options and flag any paid tool before adding it.

Can I cancel the monthly subscription?

Yes — it’s month-to-month. Cancel any month, with no notice period and no exit fee. We confirm the terms in writing before you start.

Can I hire WebsitesPixel for a single technical task?

Yes. Alongside the plans, we take on one-off Shopify tasks — a bug fix, a new section, an integration — quoted individually.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. Every build (Launch and Rebuild) includes 30 days of post-launch technical support. After that, you can move onto the Growth plan or come back for one-off tasks.

Who owns my Shopify store and its custom code?

You do. The store, its content, and the custom code we build are yours, on your Shopify account.

Can existing Shopify stores join the Growth plan?

Yes — Growth works for any live Shopify store. If your store needs a bigger overhaul first, Shopify Rebuild is often the better starting point, and you can move onto Growth afterward.

What if my project does not fit a package?

Some do not. The packages cover a defined scope so you can place yourself quickly. If yours needs more — more than 500 products, custom integrations, B2B pricing — we scope it on the call and quote it in writing before anything starts.

Do you take payment plans?

For Launch and Rebuild, 50/50 is standard: half to start, half at launch. Larger, separately quoted builds can be split across three milestones. We will not start work before the first payment, and you will not be asked for the last one before you have seen it live.

What if we are not a good fit?

We will say so on the call and point you somewhere better. That happens a few times a year and it has never once been the wrong decision.

Not sure which plan fits?

Thirty minutes. We look at what you have, tell you which plan fits and why, and you decide from there. No pitch deck.
