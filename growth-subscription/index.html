How it runs.

  1. We audit the store

    Before anything ships we go through analytics, the funnel and the theme, and come back with the list of things actually costing you revenue, ranked.

  2. You add requests to the board

    One shared board. Add what you need, order it however you like, change your mind. You can always see what is in progress and what is next.

  3. We ship, measure, repeat

    Work goes live continuously rather than in a big reveal. Anything conversion related gets measured, and we tell you when something did not work.

Questions founders actually ask.

How many requests can I have open?

As many as you like on the board. We work one or two at a time so things actually finish, and you decide the order. If something urgent lands, it goes to the top.

What if I only need a little work one month?

Pause it. You keep everything already shipped, and pick it up when the next campaign or launch comes round.

Do you work in my existing theme?

Yes. We work in whatever you are on and leave it cleaner than we found it. If the theme is genuinely holding the store back we will say so and show you why, not quietly rebuild it.

Who actually does the work?

The same small team every month, and you talk to them directly. No account manager relaying messages.

What if it is not working out?

Cancel any month. No notice period and no exit fee. Everything built for you is yours, in your Shopify account.

How is this different from your project work?

Projects are right for a full build or a migration with a defined end. The subscription is for the continuous work after that, which is where most stores lose ground.
