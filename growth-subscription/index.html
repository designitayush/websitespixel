Clear terms, stated plainly.

Growth is a working partnership, not unlimited work. Here is exactly what the $699 covers and what it does not.

How the monthly allowance works

  • Up to 15 hours of agreed work per billing month.
  • Work is prioritized through a monthly task plan.
  • All service categories are accessible within the monthly allowance.
  • One primary workstream at a time.
  • Requests are handled by priority and available capacity.
  • Additional work requires your approval and a separate quote.
  • Month-to-month. Cancel any month — no notice period and no exit fee.
  • Quiet month? Pause the plan. You keep everything already shipped and pick it up when you need it.

Not included in the $699

  • Advertising spend
  • Third-party app and software fees
  • Premium themes and external subscriptions
  • Major migrations and large standalone projects — quoted separately
  • Unlimited work — the plan is capped at 15 hours a month
  • Guaranteed rankings, traffic, sales, or revenue — nobody can honestly promise those

How it runs.

  1. We audit the store

    Before anything ships we go through analytics, the funnel and the theme, and come back with the list of things actually costing you revenue, ranked.

  2. We agree the month’s plan

    Everything you want done goes on one shared board. Each month we agree what fits within your hours, in your order of priority. You can always see what is in progress and what is next.

  3. We ship, measure, repeat

    Work goes live through the month rather than in a big reveal. Anything conversion related gets measured, and we tell you when something did not work.

Questions founders actually ask.

How many requests can I have open?

Everything you want done goes on one shared board. Each month we agree which items fit within your 15 hours and work through them one primary workstream at a time, in the order you choose. If something urgent lands, it goes to the top of the plan.

What happens if a month needs more than 15 hours?

We scope the extra work and quote it for your approval first. Nothing beyond the allowance is billed without your yes.

What if I only need a little work one month?

Pause it. You keep everything already shipped, and pick it up when the next campaign or launch comes round.

Do you work in my existing theme?

Yes. We work in whatever you are on and leave it cleaner than we found it. If the theme is genuinely holding the store back we will say so and show you why, not quietly rebuild it.

Who actually does the work?

The same small team every month, and you talk to them directly. No account manager relaying messages.

What if it is not working out?

Cancel any month. No notice period and no exit fee. Everything built for you is yours, in your Shopify account.

How is this different from your project work?

Shopify Launch and Shopify Rebuild are fixed-price projects with a defined end. Growth is for the continuous work after that, which is where most stores lose ground.
