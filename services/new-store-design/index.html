02 Why it happens

Most new stores fail commercially not because they are ugly but because nobody decided what the pages had to argue. A homepage that does not say what you sell in five seconds, a product page that answers questions in catalogue order rather than the order buyers ask them, a checkout with three unnecessary steps. We design the argument before the interface, which is why the result converts rather than merely photographs well.
