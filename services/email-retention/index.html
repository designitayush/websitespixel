02 Why it happens

Retention is not an email problem, it is a timing problem. The winback that arrives on day thirty for a product with a sixty-day cycle is early, and the customer reads it as noise. Flows have to match how the product is actually consumed, which means looking at your order data before writing a single subject line. And a brand that only ever emails a discount has taught its customers exactly what to wait for.
