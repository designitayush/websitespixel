What we actually look at.

The same checklist we run before quoting any project.

First impression

What a cold visitor understands in five seconds, and whether the offer is clear enough to act on.

Product pages

Imagery, information order, objection handling and how hard it is to reach add to cart.

Cart and checkout

Where people drop, what is unnecessary, and anything that quietly adds friction.

Mobile

Where most of your traffic is and where most stores are weakest. Reviewed on a real handset.

Speed

Core Web Vitals, what is slowing the store, and which of it is worth fixing first.

Theme health

App sprawl, leftover code and anything that will make future changes expensive.

Analytics

Whether you can actually see your funnel, or are guessing from top line numbers.

Technical SEO

Indexing, structure and the basics that decide whether you earn traffic or rent it.

Before you ask.

Is this really free?

Yes. It takes us a couple of hours and it is how we decide whether we are a good fit for each other. Plenty of people take the audit and fix things themselves.

Is it automated?

No. Automated scores tell you your images are large. A person goes through the store and tells you which specific decisions are costing you orders.

Do I need to give you access?

Not for the audit. The public storefront is enough. If you want the analytics section to be specific, read-only GA4 access helps, but it is optional.

What happens afterwards?

You get the walkthrough and the list. If you want us to fix it we will quote it. If you do not, that is genuinely fine and you will not be chased.

Would you rather just talk it through?

Book a 30 minute call instead. Same outcome, faster, and you can ask questions as we go.
